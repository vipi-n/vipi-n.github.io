About

Engineer by craft. Problem-solver by nature.

My story

I’m drawn to the parts of backend engineering that only become visible when a system is busy: ordering, failure recovery, observability, and making the next change safe.

Across eight years and several domains, the work has changed but the thread has stayed consistent—turning operational complexity into dependable services and clean interfaces.

I care about how software behaves at 2 a.m., how the code reads six months later, and whether another engineer can extend it without needing a map. Good systems should be resilient in production and understandable in a pull request.

Lately, I’ve also been exploring custom AI agents and assisted engineering workflows as a practical way to shorten feedback loops, sharpen reviews, and automate repetitive checks.
